§ 01 — Shopping

Tax Refund
in Korea

TL;DR
Spend ₩30,000+ in one store → get the VAT refund slip → claim cash at the airport before departing.
At the store
  1. 01Spend ₩30,000 or more in a single store.
  2. 02Tell the cashier "Tax refund please" and show your passport.
  3. 03Receive the tax refund envelope / receipt — keep it safe.
At the airport (ICN)
  1. 01If total purchases exceed ₩750,000 — get a customs stamp before departing the arrivals area.
  2. 02After security, find a Tax Refund counter (Global Blue, KT&G, etc.).
  3. 03Get your refund in cash or back to your card.
Japan Tax Free
In Japan, spend ¥5,000+ in one store and show your passport at checkout — tax is removed on the spot. No airport step needed (but don't use the items before leaving Japan).
Common stores that offer it

Olive Young, Lotte Duty Free, Shinsegae Duty Free, Musinsa, most large malls and shopping centres.

§ 02 — Before You Go

Before
You Go

  1. 01Enable international use on your card. Call your bank or do it in the app before departing — some cards block foreign transactions by default.
  2. 02Check your power bank size. Flights prohibit power banks over 100Wh (≈27,000 mAh). Anything larger must be declared or may be confiscated.
  3. 03Download Naver Map. Google Maps transit is unreliable in Korea. Naver Map works offline and has full English support.
  4. 04Download KakaoMap as a backup — also excellent for navigation and finding restaurants.
Card tip
Revolut or Wise are ideal — no foreign transaction fees and great exchange rates. Load some balance before the trip.
