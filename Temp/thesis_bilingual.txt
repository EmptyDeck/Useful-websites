EN: Detection Early Enough to Resume: A SAM3-Based Pipeline for 3D-Print Bed-Adhesion Failure Onset. Sejik Park. Abstract— Existing 3D-print failure monitors detect spaghetti — the visible tangle of extruded filament that forms after bed adhesion has already failed and the partial print has been lost. We re-define the detection target around the earlier event, the failure onset, and require detection inside the resumability window: the interval during which an operator could still refix the partial print and resume the job. The mask of a print object under a bed-synchronised camera is pixel-stationary by construction during normal operation, so the problem reduces to detecting the moment the mask becomes unstable.
